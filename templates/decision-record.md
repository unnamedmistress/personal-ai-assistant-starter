# My decision record

## Decision and date

State whether this is proposed or decided. Record the owner and date.

## Choice and reason

Describe what you chose and the evidence behind it.

## Assumptions

Name the facts you assumed and what is still unknown.

## Alternative declined

Record the strongest alternative and why you did not choose it.

## Reopen when

Name the new evidence that would justify revisiting this decision. A new conversation alone does not change the decision.
