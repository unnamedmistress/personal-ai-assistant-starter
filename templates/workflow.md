# My assistant workflow

## The job

Describe one real task and the useful result you want.

## Inputs and privacy

Name the sources the assistant may use. Record which information must stay out of AI tools or public files.

## My decisions

State what only you may decide or approve. Name actions the assistant must not take.

## Finished means

Describe the artifact or result and the evidence needed to check it. If delivery is not part of the task, do not require or imply a send.

## If something is missing
