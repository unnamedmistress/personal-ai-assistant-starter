# A possible lesson

## Observation

Record what happened, when, and the source. Start with candidate status rather than treating one event as a rule.

## Other explanations

Name what else could explain the result and any conflicting evidence.

## Independent checks

Record separate confirming events and who checked their relevance and independence. Repeated summaries of one event do not count as separate events.

## Proposed change and owner review

Describe the change you want to make. Record whether the owner approved that exact change.

## Test and retirement

Describe how you will check the change and what evidence would cause you to revise or retire it. Preserve the prior record when the lesson changes.
